## 3. Page Copy, Section by Section

### Hero
- **Eyebrow:** NOW OPEN · SECTOR 23, TALOJA PHASE 2 · BEHIND PENDHAR METRO
- **H1:** Maternity Hospital & Lady Gynecologist in Taloja. *Everything Under One Roof.*
- **Sub:** From your first pregnancy scan to your baby's first vaccine, every step happens inside one building — with NICU, ICU, sonography and 24/7 emergency care on-site. Led by **Dr. Vaibhavi Birle**, MBBS, MS Obstetrics & Gynaecology, who explains every step until you understand.
- **Chips:** Lady Gynecologist · NICU & ICU On-Site · 24/7 Emergency · Govt. Recognised MTP & TL Centre · Mediclaim Accepted
- **CTAs:** Free Pregnancy Check-Up Every Thursday · 📞 Call 7039330945
- **Address:** Ridhisha Tower, Sector 23 — Behind Pendhar Metro Station · Near Prathmesh Society · Taloja Phase 2, Navi Mumbai 410 208

> **Why:** The old H1 ("Safe Delivery. Complete Women's Care.") carried zero search terms. Google weighs the H1 heavily, so it now holds the two Tier-1 keywords. The emotional promise moves into the italic second line so the hero still reads warm. "Near Prathmesh Society" captures the landmark search.

**H1 alternatives:**
- A: *Maternity Hospital & Lady Gynecologist in Taloja. Everything Under One Roof.* — used; both Tier-1 keywords plus the brand's core promise
- B: *Lady Gynecologist & Maternity Hospital near Pendhar Metro.* — stronger for the metro micro-geography, weaker for the broad "Taloja" search
- C: *Safe Delivery in Taloja, with NICU & ICU in the Same Building.* — leads with the #1 fear, but drops "lady gynecologist"
