### Card 2: Painless Delivery

**Painless Delivery — Epidural Labour Care**

You can have a natural delivery with significantly less pain. Epidural analgesia — a pain relief procedure given during active labour — allows your body to deliver your baby while keeping you comfortable and in control.

Dr. Vaibhavi will explain exactly what this involves before you decide.

→ *Learn more about Delivery Options*

---

### Card 3: PCOD / PCOS Management

**PCOD and PCOS Treatment**

Irregular periods. Unexplained weight gain. Hair loss. Difficulty conceiving. These are not things you should just "manage." They are signs your body is asking for help.

PCOD (Polycystic Ovarian Disease) is common — and treatable. Dr. Vaibhavi will explain your condition clearly and guide you through a plan that works for your body.

→ *Learn more about Women's Health Care*

---

### Card 4: Antenatal Care (ANC)

**Pregnancy Check-Ups — From the First Trimester Onwards**

ANC, or Antenatal Care, is the regular check-up and monitoring your baby needs throughout pregnancy. At Vaibhavi Hospital, your ANC visits include a full review of your health, your baby's growth, diet guidance, and answers to every question you carry into the room.

→ *Learn more about Antenatal Care*

---

### Card 5: Infertility Treatment

**Infertility — A Sensitive Start, With Expert Care**

If you have been trying to conceive without success, you deserve clear answers — not vague reassurance. At Vaibhavi Hospital, Dr. Vaibhavi will guide you through a complete evaluation and a step-by-step treatment plan.

→ *Learn more about Infertility Care*

### Card 6: Family Planning
