## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 June 2026

Your trust matters as much as your care. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

### 1. Who we are

Vaibhavi Hospital is a women's and maternity hospital at Ridhisha Tower, Sector 23, Behind Pendhar Metro Station, Taloja Phase 2, Navi Mumbai - 410208. This policy covers our website, vaibhavihospitaltaloja.in.

### 2. What we collect

When you request an appointment on our website, we ask for:

- Your name
- Your phone number
- The service you are enquiring about (optional)

That is all. Your medical records, reports and payment details are handled at the hospital, in person, under the laws that govern medical records.

### 3. How we use it

Your details are used only to:

- Call you back to confirm your appointment
- Answer your questions about our services
- Guide you on what to bring for your visit

### 4. Your information stays with us

Your details are seen only by the Vaibhavi Hospital team arranging your appointment: our front desk and your doctor. We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.

We share information outside the hospital only when the law requires it, for example a court order or a request from a public health authority.

### 5. How we protect it

Appointment enquiries are accessible only to authorised hospital staff. We use reasonable security measures to keep your information safe from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

### 6. How long we keep it

We keep appointment enquiries for as long as we need them to arrange your care and meet legal record-keeping requirements. After that, they are securely deleted.

### 7. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

- Tell you what information we hold about you
- Correct anything that is wrong
- Delete your information, where the law allows
- Stop contacting you

Call or email us, and our team will help you.

### 8. Cookies and website tools

Our website may use basic cookies to load pages correctly and to understand how visitors use the site. You can manage cookies anytime in your browser settings.

Our website uses Google Fonts, and our WhatsApp and Google Maps links open those services. Each follows its own privacy policy.

### 9. Young patients

Adolescent girls are welcome at Vaibhavi Hospital. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to fill in the appointment form with you.

### 10. Updates to this policy

When this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

## Notes for Review

**Voice choices**
- The intro opens with the patient ("Your trust matters as much as your care") rather than "We take your privacy seriously," which is a cliché every site uses.
- Plain headings like "Your information stays with us" and "Your choices" replace legal headings like "Disclosure to Third Parties" and "Data Subject Rights." Same substance, easier to read.
- Section 9 uses the gentle adolescent tone from the brand voice guide and frames the rule as a welcome.

**Zero Negatives Standard**
Applied everywhere except one line: "We never sell, rent or trade your personal information." This is the single sentence patients look for in a privacy policy, and a positive rewrite would weaken it. Recommend keeping it.

**Confirm before publishing**
1. **Legal review.** India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 expects a named contact for privacy complaints (a grievance officer). Ask Dr. Vaibhavi who this should be, and have a lawyer review the final text.
2. **Analytics.** Section 8 assumes basic cookies only. If Google Analytics or a Meta pixel is added to the site, this section needs updating.
3. **Form email.** Confirm where appointment form submissions go (the email address is still an open item in CLAUDE.md).
4. **Email address.** info@vaibhavihospitaltaloja.in is still unconfirmed.
5. **Effective date.** Set to the inauguration date, 13 June 2026. Change it to the actual publish date if the page goes live later.
