### Card 1: High-Risk Pregnancy

**High-Risk Pregnancy — Managed Here, Start to Finish**

When a pregnancy carries additional risk — because of high blood pressure, diabetes, a previous C-section, or any other concern — your care requires a hospital with full on-site capability.

Vaibhavi Hospital's ICU, NICU, and Surgical Readiness mean your care remains entirely within this building. Obstetric Continuity is maintained from your first antenatal check-up through delivery and post-delivery care — for both you and your newborn.

→ *Pregnancy Care at Vaibhavi Hospital*

---

### Card 2: Painless Delivery

**Painless Delivery — Epidural Pain Relief During Labour**

You can deliver your baby naturally with significantly less pain. An epidural — a safe pain-relief injection given during active labour — means you stay comfortable and present while your baby arrives. You are awake. You are in control. You feel less pain.

Before any decision, you will know exactly what the procedure involves. That is the standard at Vaibhavi Hospital.

→ *Delivery Options at Vaibhavi Hospital*

---

### Card 3: PCOD / PCOS Management

**PCOD and PCOS — A Real Plan. A Clear Diagnosis.**

Irregular periods. Unexplained weight gain. Hair loss. Difficulty conceiving. These are signals your body is sending — and they deserve a real, clinical answer.

PCOD, or Polycystic Ovarian Disease, is common and fully manageable. At Vaibhavi Hospital, you leave your first consultation with a clear diagnosis and a treatment plan you fully understand.

→ *Women's Health at Vaibhavi Hospital*

---

### Card 4: Antenatal Care (ANC)

**Pregnancy Check-Ups — From the First Month to Delivery Day**

ANC, or Antenatal Care, is the series of check-ups that keep you and your baby safe from the first trimester through delivery. At Vaibhavi Hospital, each visit covers your health, your baby's growth, nutrition guidance, and answers to every question you carry into the room.

Your ANC visits and your diagnostic tests happen in the same building — Obstetric Continuity in practice, from week one to delivery day.

→ *Antenatal Care at Vaibhavi Hospital*

---

### Card 5: Infertility Treatment

**Infertility — Clear Answers First. Then a Step-By-Step Plan.**

When you have been trying to conceive, you deserve a structured clinical evaluation and a specific, actionable plan. At Vaibhavi Hospital, the first step is understanding exactly what is happening. The second step is a care plan you can follow, explained in full.

→ *Infertility Care at Vaibhavi Hospital*

---

### Card 6: Family Planning

**Family Planning — Government Recognised. Explained in Full.**

Whether you need guidance on contraception, a Copper-T fitting, or a permanent family planning procedure like Tubal Ligation — Vaibhavi Hospital is a Government Recognised MTP (Medical Termination of Pregnancy) & TL (Tubal Ligation) Centre, with clinical protocols that meet state safety standards.

Every procedure is safe, regulated, and explained in full before you consent.

→ *Family Planning at Vaibhavi Hospital*
