## Services Grid

### High-Risk Pregnancy
When your pregnancy needs closer attention, you need to know that everything required is already here — and it is. Clinical Management of hypertensive disorders, gestational diabetes, foetal growth restriction, and obstetric emergencies, with on-site ICU and NICU readiness at every stage.

### Painless Delivery
The day you meet your baby should be the most supported day of your life — whether vaginal or LSCS (Caesarean delivery). Clinical Management of labour and delivery, including epidural pain relief and full surgical readiness for Caesarean delivery, on-site.

### PCOD / PCOS
Your body has been telling you something for months — irregular cycles, weight shifts, skin and hair changes — and you deserve answers, clearly explained. Clinical Management of Polycystic Ovarian Disease and Syndrome through hormonal assessment, lifestyle counselling, and medication protocols.

### ANC Care (Antenatal Care)
Every check-up, every growth scan, every question across your entire pregnancy — guided by the same doctor, from your first visit to the day you deliver. Clinical Management of antenatal health through trimester-by-trimester monitoring, nutritional guidance, and personalised birth planning.

### Infertility
If the wait has been hard and the answers have been unclear, you are in the right place — with a doctor who listens, keeps trying, and explains every step. Clinical Management of primary and secondary infertility through diagnostic evaluation, ovulation induction, hormonal therapy, and surgical intervention where required.

### Family Planning
The right decision is yours — we make sure you have everything you need to make it with confidence and clarity. Clinical Management of contraceptive planning, Copper-T insertion, MTP (Medical Termination of Pregnancy), and TL (Tubectomy/Tubal Ligation) at a Govt. Recognised centre.
