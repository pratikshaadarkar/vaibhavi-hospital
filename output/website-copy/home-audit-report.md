## AUDIT 4 — Institutional vs. Personal Framing
### Is "explains clearly" a personal quirk or The Vaibhavi Standard of Care?

**Findings: This is the most significant structural issue in the draft.**

The current Section 4 frames explanation as something Dr. Vaibhavi personally does. The section label says "MEET YOUR DOCTOR." The body says "she explains again. That is simply how she works."

This is accurate — but it positions explanation as Dr. Vaibhavi's personality, not Vaibhavi Hospital's institutional standard. The risk: if the reader's brain files this under "this doctor is nice," it does not become a reason to choose the *hospital* when Dr. Vaibhavi is on leave or the hospital scales.

The fix: Reframe explanation as The Vaibhavi Standard — a promise the institution makes, embodied by the doctor who founded it.

**Specific rewrites required:**

| Current | Revised framing |
|---|---|
| Section label: "MEET YOUR DOCTOR" | "THE VAIBHAVI STANDARD OF CARE" |
| "That is simply how she works." | "At Vaibhavi Hospital, no patient leaves without understanding what happened and what comes next. That is the standard — for every consultation, every visit." |
| "She started Vaibhavi Hospital because she wanted to give every woman in Taloja access to care that is complete, affordable, and genuinely human." | Keep — this is the bridge from personal to institutional. Strengthen the landing: "She built this hospital so that standard becomes yours — every time you walk in." |
| Section 5 card language: "Dr. Vaibhavi will explain..." (×3 cards) | At least two cards should say "At Vaibhavi Hospital, you will be explained..." or "Before any decision, you will know exactly what it involves." Preserve one "Dr. Vaibhavi will explain" for human warmth. |

## AUDIT 5 — Jargon & Complexity Audit
### Sentences that sound like a government health notice or medical textbook.

**Flagged sentences and rewrites:**

**Flag 1 — Section 5, Card 2:**
> "Epidural analgesia — a pain relief procedure given during active labour — allows your body to deliver your baby while keeping you comfortable and in control."

*Assessment:* "Epidural analgesia" + "active labour" = two multi-syllable clinical terms in one sentence. The sentence is almost right but the phrase "allows your body to deliver" is slightly passive.
*Rewrite:* "Epidural — a safe pain-relief injection given during labour — means you stay comfortable and in control while your baby arrives. You are awake. You are present. You just feel less pain."

---

**Flag 2 — Section 5, Card 4:**
> "ANC, or Antenatal Care, is the regular check-up and monitoring your baby needs throughout pregnancy."

*Assessment:* Acceptable Plain English. But "monitoring" + "throughout pregnancy" is slightly clinical. The second sentence rescues it well. Keep with minor tightening.
*Rewrite:* "ANC, or Antenatal Care, is the series of check-ups that keep you and your baby safe from the first month to delivery day."

---

**Flag 3 — Section 5, Card 6:**
> "Whether you need contraceptive guidance, a Copper-T (IUCD) insertion, or information about permanent family planning procedures like Tubal Ligation..."

*Assessment:* Three clinical terms (Copper-T, IUCD, Tubal Ligation) in one sentence. Reads like a service catalogue. "IUCD" is a medical acronym that adds no value for the reader since "Copper-T" is already the common name.
*Rewrite:* "Whether you need guidance on contraception, a Copper-T fitting, or a permanent family planning procedure like Tubal Ligation (TL) — Vaibhavi Hospital is a Government Recognised centre for all of these."

---

**Flag 4 — Section 4, Credential strip:**
> "Govt. Recognised MTP & TL Centre"

*Assessment:* This is a factual designation — appropriate in a credential strip. But "MTP & TL" without expansion is hospital-speak for a non-clinical reader. Add one-line explanation when this appears in body copy (not needed in the strip itself).
*Action:* In Section 5 Card 6 and Section 6 footer strip, write it as: "Government Recognised MTP (Medical Termination of Pregnancy) & TL (Tubal Ligation) Centre."

---

**Flag 5 — Trust Bar, label:**
> "Operation Theatre — LSCS & Gynaecological Surgeries"

*Assessment:* "LSCS" is unlabelled here. First mention rule — must be explained.
*Rewrite:* "Operation Theatre — LSCS (Caesarean Delivery) & Gynaecological Surgeries"
