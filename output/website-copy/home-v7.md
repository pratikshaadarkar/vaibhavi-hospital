**Card 1 — Painless Delivery**
Your comfort during labour is your right — a fully supported birth, planned around you and your baby.
Clinical Management of labour and delivery, including epidural pain relief and full surgical readiness for LSCS (Caesarean delivery), on-site.

**Card 2 — High-Risk Pregnancy**
When your pregnancy needs closer attention, everything it requires is already here — and so is your doctor.
Clinical Management of hypertensive disorders, gestational diabetes, foetal growth restriction, and obstetric emergencies with on-site ICU and NICU readiness.

**Card 3 — NICU & ICU On-Site**
Your newborn's intensive care and your own critical care — both fully staffed, inside this building, from the first day.
Clinical Management of neonatal and adult intensive care needs, on-site, with zero transfer requirement.

**Card 4 — PCOD & PCOS**
Your body has been telling you something — and you deserve answers, clearly explained, with a treatment plan you understand.
Clinical Management of Polycystic Ovarian Disease and Syndrome through hormonal assessment, lifestyle counselling, and medication protocols.

**Card 5 — All Diagnostics Here**
Sonography, pathology, radiology — on the ground floor of this building. Walk down, get your reports, walk back up.
Integrated Diagnostic Precision through Mekhale Diagnostic Center: obstetric ultrasound, colour Doppler, X-ray, and pathology lab — same day, same building.

**Card 6 — Infertility Care**
A doctor who listens, keeps trying, and explains every step — because this journey deserves that.
Clinical Management of primary and secondary infertility through diagnostic evaluation, ovulation induction, hormonal therapy, and surgical intervention where required.
