`[White background. Two columns on desktop. Left: the pain. Right: the resolution. Mobile: stacks vertically.]`

### Left column — The Pain

**If you have ever felt this, you are not alone.**

You've been pregnant and found out your hospital doesn't do sonography. So you went elsewhere for the scan, came back for the consultation, then had to go again for blood tests — all while you were already tired and anxious.

Or you've been told by another hospital: *"We can handle the delivery, but if something goes wrong, we'll have to transfer you."*

That is not something any family should hear during the most important day of their lives.

### Right column — The Resolution

**At Vaibhavi Hospital, the answer to both of those fears is the same: everything stays here.**

Vaibhavi Hospital is designed around a Strategic Diagnostic Layer — Mekhale Diagnostic Center occupies the ground floor of Ridhisha Tower, the same building as the hospital. Your sonography, blood tests, and pathology happen downstairs. You walk back up with your results. No travel. No separate registration. No extra cost.

Your delivery happens here at our maternity hospital in Taloja Phase 2. If your newborn needs extra care after birth, our NICU provides Neonatal Stabilization on-site — your baby does not leave the building, and neither do you.

Obstetric Continuity — care for mother and baby under one roof, without a single handover to another facility — is not a feature we offer. It is the structure we were built on.

No referrals. No transfers. No running between buildings.

### Card 2: Painless Delivery

**Painless Delivery — Epidural Pain Relief During Labour**

You can deliver your baby naturally with significantly less pain. An epidural — a safe pain-relief injection given during active labour — means you stay comfortable and present while your baby arrives. You are awake. You are in control. You just feel less pain.

Before any decision, you will know exactly what the procedure involves. That is the standard at Vaibhavi Hospital.

→ *Delivery Options at Vaibhavi Hospital*

---

### Card 3: PCOD / PCOS Management

**PCOD and PCOS — Not Just a Prescription. A Plan.**

Irregular periods. Unexplained weight gain. Hair loss. Difficulty conceiving. These are not things to just push through. They are signals your body is sending — and they deserve a real answer.

PCOD, or Polycystic Ovarian Disease, is common and fully manageable. At Vaibhavi Hospital, you will leave your first consultation with a clear diagnosis and a treatment plan you understand — not just a prescription slip.

→ *Women's Health at Vaibhavi Hospital*

---

### Card 4: Antenatal Care (ANC)

**Pregnancy Check-Ups — From the First Month to Delivery Day**

ANC, or Antenatal Care, is the series of check-ups that keep you and your baby safe from the first trimester through delivery. At Vaibhavi Hospital, each visit covers your health, your baby's growth, nutrition guidance, and answers to every question you carry into the room.

Your ANC visits and your diagnostic tests happen in the same building. That is Obstetric Continuity in practice.

→ *Antenatal Care at Vaibhavi Hospital*
