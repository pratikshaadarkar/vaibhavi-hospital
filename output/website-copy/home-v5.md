## Services Grid

### High-Risk Pregnancy
Your pregnancy deserves the full clinical attention it requires — right here, at every stage. Clinical Management of hypertensive disorders, gestational diabetes, foetal growth restriction, and obstetric emergencies with on-site ICU and NICU readiness.

### Painless Delivery
A safe, supported delivery — whether vaginal or LSCS (Caesarean delivery) — planned around your health and your baby's. Clinical Management of labour, including epidural pain relief options and full surgical readiness for Caesarean delivery, on-site.

### PCOD / PCOS
Irregular periods, weight changes, skin concerns — your body is giving you signals, and we help you understand every one. Clinical Management of Polycystic Ovarian Disease and Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome through hormonal assessment, lifestyle guidance, and medication protocols.

### ANC Care (Antenatal Care)
Your full pregnancy journey — from your first confirmation scan to your delivery day — guided and monitored in one place. Clinical Management of antenatal health through scheduled check-ups, growth scans, nutritional counselling, and birth planning across all three trimesters.

### Infertility
If you have been trying to conceive and need answers, you are in the right place. Clinical Management of primary and secondary infertility through diagnostic evaluation, ovulation induction, hormonal therapy, and surgical intervention where required.

### Family Planning
The right choice for your family is one only you can make — we give you the information and the options to make it clearly. Clinical Management of contraceptive planning, Copper-T insertion, MTP (Medical Termination of Pregnancy), and TL (Tubectomy/Tubal Ligation) at a Govt. Recognised centre.
