Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 01 November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal information in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA), Information Technology Act 2000, and other applicable Indian laws.

1. Introduction

Vaibhavi Hospital ("we," "our," or "us") operates the https://vaibhavihospital.in/ website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal information in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA), Information Technology Act 2000, Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules 2011, and other applicable Indian laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number. We may collect this information when you submit the appointment request or contact form on our website, or when you contact us by phone or email.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving our services
  • Sending promotional communications (with your explicit consent)
  • Responding to your inquiries and support requests
  • Analysing usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Fraud prevention and security

4. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except as required by law or with your explicit consent.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not set its own cookies. However, it uses third-party services such as Google Fonts, an embedded Google Map and a Jotform widget, which may set cookies or collect technical data under their own privacy policies. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable law. You can request deletion of your data at any time, subject to legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (including encryption, access controls, and secure servers) to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erase your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to grievance redressal
  • Right to nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity

To exercise these rights, please contact us at vaibhavi.hospital.taloja@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. Under DPDPA 2023, collection of data from children requires verifiable parental consent. If we become aware we have collected data from a minor without proper consent, we will delete such data immediately.

10. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, where required by law, by sending an email notification. Your continued use of the Service after notification constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Contact Us and Grievance Redressal

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Information Technology Act 2000, we have designated a Grievance Officer to address your privacy concerns. Please direct all queries and grievances to:

Vaibhavi Hospital

Email: vaibhavi.hospital.taloja@gmail.com

Website: https://vaibhavihospital.in/

We will acknowledge your grievance within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days of receipt.
