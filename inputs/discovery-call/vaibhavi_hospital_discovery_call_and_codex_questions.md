# PART A | Website & Copywriting Discovery Call

Questions to gather everything needed to build the website and write the copy.

## 1. Business & Hospital Overview

- What is the full name of the hospital and what does Vaibhavi mean to you?
- How long have you been running the hospital in Taloja?
- Which services do you currently offer? (Maternity, X-Ray, Sonography, Diagnosis, OPD)
- Are you the only doctor or do you have visiting consultants?
- What are your OPD timings and days?

---

## 2. Services to Feature on the Website

- Which services do you want highlighted most on the website?
- Do you offer antenatal care packages? If yes, what is included?
- Do you handle normal deliveries, C-sections, or both?
- What diagnostic tests are available in-house?
- Are there any upcoming services you plan to add?

---

## 3. About the Doctor

- What are your qualifications and years of experience?
- Where did you study and complete your training?
- Why did you choose gynaecology as your speciality?
- Why did you open your own hospital in Taloja specifically?

---

## 4. Target Patients

- Who is your ideal patient? (Pregnant women, families, working women, general OPD?)
- Which areas do most of your patients come from? (Taloja, Kharghar, Panvel, Ulwe?)
- What is the typical age group you treat most?
- Do patients come on referral or do they find you on their own?

---

## 5. Trust & Credibility

- How many patients or deliveries have you handled till date (approximate)?
- Do you have any certifications or registrations? (Maharashtra Medical Council, etc.)
- Do you have patient testimonials or Google reviews we can use?
- Have you conducted any health camps or community events?
- Can we showcase any recovery stories with patient permission?

---

## 6. Content & Visual Assets

- Do you have a professional logo and brand colours finalized?
- Do you have photos of the hospital? (Reception, wards, OT, equipment, exterior)
- Do you have a professional photo of yourself for the website?
- Do you have any printed brochures or package PDFs we can refer to?
- Are you open to a short photoshoot if needed?

---

## 7. Website Goals & Structure

- What is the one action you want every website visitor to take? (Call, WhatsApp, book appointment?)
- What pages do you want? (Home, About, Services, Gallery, Testimonials, Contact?)
- Do you want an online appointment booking form?
- Should the website be in English, Hindi, Marathi, or a mix?
- Do you want a blog or health tips section?
- Is there any website you like the look or feel of?

---

## 8. Competition & Positioning

- Who are the other hospitals or clinics nearby that patients might consider?
- What makes patients choose you over them?
- Are you positioned as affordable, mid-range, or premium?

---

## 9. Brand Voice & Tone

- How do you want patients to feel when they land on your website? (Safe, cared for, confident, trusted?)
- How would you describe your own personality as a doctor? (Warm? Expert? Calm?)
- What should the website never sound like? (Too corporate? Too cold? Too jargon-heavy?)
- Any words or phrases you want used or specifically avoided?

---

## 10. Technical & Practical

- Do you already have a domain name?
- Do you have existing hosting or should we set it up fresh?
- Who will manage and update the website after it goes live?
- Do you need WhatsApp chat integration on the website?
- Do you need Google Maps embedded?
- What is your budget for the website?
- What is your expected go-live timeline?

# 3 Things to Pay Close Attention to During the Call

### 1. Their Language
The exact words the doctor uses to describe her patients and her care is your best raw copy.

### 2. Their Pride
What they are most proud of is usually the strongest headline on the homepage.

### 3. Their Patient
The more specific she is about who she treats, the sharper and more targeted the copy will be.

# PART B | Customer Codex Questions

Questions to understand the emotional and psychological journey of your patient.

Answers from this section will feed directly into your copywriting tone, headlines, and messaging.

## 1. Fears & Desires

- What is a patient's biggest fear when choosing a new hospital for delivery or treatment? (Safety, hidden costs, being ignored, inexperienced staff?)
- What do pregnant women or families worry about most throughout the treatment journey?
- Beyond treatment, what do patients truly want when they walk into your hospital? (To feel safe, to be heard, to trust completely?)
- What does the perfect hospital experience look like in a patient's mind?

---

## 2. Triggers & Decision Moment

- What is the one thing that makes a patient or family finally say, "Yes, we will come here"?
- What doubts or hesitations do patients express right before confirming, and how do you handle them?
- Is there a specific moment — first call, first scan, first visit — where patients say they fully trusted you?
- Do most patients decide based on someone's recommendation or on their own research?

---

## 3. Their Language

- What exact words do patients or families use when they first call or walk in? (Marathi, Hindi, or English — word for word)
- What compliments do happy patients give after a safe delivery or successful treatment? (Their words, not yours)
- What complaints have you heard about other hospitals in the area that brought patients to you?
- What questions do pregnant women ask most often during antenatal visits?

---

## 4. Stories & Credibility

- Tell me one patient story you are genuinely proud of — a case that was difficult, emotional, or memorable.
- Why did you start your own hospital in Taloja? What personal experience led to this decision?
- What is one thing about the way you treat patients that most hospitals or doctors do not do?
- Have any patients come back to you specifically for a second delivery or referred family members?

---

## 5. Brand Humanity

- If Vaibhavi Hospital were a person, how would you describe their personality? (Warm elder sister? Calm expert? Trusted neighbourhood doctor?)
- What emotion should a patient feel the moment they open your website? (Safe? Relieved? Confident? Welcomed?)
- How do you want women patients in particular to feel when they read about you?

---

# How to Use This After the Call

Once you have all answers, compile them into a Customer Codex document using these sections:

| Section | What Goes In It |
|----------|----------------|
| Dream Patient Profile | Who they are + their fears and life situation |
| Fears & Objections | What stops them from choosing this hospital |
| Desires & Dream State | What they truly want — safety, care, trust |
| Trigger Moments | What makes them finally decide to visit |
| Their Language | Exact Marathi/Hindi/English words they use |
| Brand Voice & Stories | Doctor's personality + memorable patient stories |
| Journey Map | Awareness → First Visit → Trust → Loyal Patient |
