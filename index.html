Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about delivery, NICU, Mediclaim, timings and the free Thursday check-up at Vaibhavi Hospital, Taloja.

Is there a gynecologist at Vaibhavi Hospital in Taloja?

Yes. Vaibhavi Hospital is led by Dr. Vaibhavi Birle, MBBS, MS Obstetrics & Gynaecology, with 8+ years of experience in pregnancy care, delivery, PCOD, infertility and menopause. Every patient is seen by a female gynecologist.

Does Vaibhavi Hospital have a NICU and ICU?

Yes. The NICU (with newborn warmer) and the ICU (with oxygen supply) are both inside the hospital building in Taloja Phase 2. If your baby or you need intensive care, it happens here, in the same building where you delivered.

Can I get sonography and blood tests in the same building?

Yes. Mekhale Diagnostic Center is on the ground floor of Ridhisha Tower, the same building as the hospital. Pregnancy sonography, follicular monitoring, X-ray and pathology tests are done downstairs, and you walk back up with your reports.

Is Mediclaim accepted at Vaibhavi Hospital?

Our TPA and cashless insurance helpdesk will be operational very soon. Please confirm with the hospital directly before admission.

What are the OPD timings?

The hospital is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. For emergencies, call +91 70393 30945. To book, call or walk in during OPD hours.

Is the Thursday pregnancy check-up really free?

Yes. Every Thursday from 5 PM to 7 PM, pregnant women can walk in for a free check-up and follow-up. Bring your previous reports and your questions.

Is Vaibhavi Hospital a government recognised MTP centre?

Yes. Vaibhavi Hospital is a Government Recognised MTP (Medical Termination of Pregnancy) and TL (Tubal Ligation) Centre, so every procedure follows state-approved clinical protocols.

Where exactly is Vaibhavi Hospital?

Ridhisha Tower, Sector 23, behind Pendhar Metro Station, Taloja Phase 2, Navi Mumbai 410 208. The nearest landmark is Prathmesh Society.
