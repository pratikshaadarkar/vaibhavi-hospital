## 1. Core OBG Specialities (Dr. Vaibhavi Birle)

These are the primary services led by Dr. Vaibhavi personally.

| Service | Detail |
|---|---|
| **ANC Care / Antenatal Care** | Full pregnancy monitoring from first trimester through delivery. Includes diet guidance, routine check-ups, and complication screening. |
| **Post-Delivery Care** | Mother and newborn care after normal delivery or LSCS. |
| **High-Risk Pregnancy** | Management of pregnancies with complications — hypertension, diabetes, multiple pregnancy, previous C-section, placenta previa, etc. |
| **Painless Delivery (Epidural)** | Labour analgesia / epidural pain management for normal vaginal delivery. |
| **Caesarean Section (LSCS)** | Lower segment caesarean section — elective and emergency. |
| **PCOD / PCOS Management** | Hormonal and lifestyle-based management of Polycystic Ovarian Disease / Syndrome. |
| **Family Planning / Copper-T IUCD** | Contraceptive counselling and insertion of intrauterine contraceptive devices. |
| **Infertility Management** | Infertility work-up and initial treatment. Specialist referral available on panel for advanced cases. |
| **Menopause Management** | Hormonal support and symptomatic care for perimenopausal and menopausal women. |
| **Menstrual Disorder Treatment** | Diagnosis and treatment of irregular, heavy, or painful periods. |
| **Colposcopy** | Cervical examination procedure for detection of pre-cancerous changes. |
| **Adolescent Counselling** | Reproductive health guidance for teenage girls — periods, hygiene, hormonal issues. |
| **Laparoscopic Surgeries** | Minimally invasive gynaecological surgeries (visiting laparoscopic surgeon on panel). |
| **Govt. Recognised MTP Centre** | Medical Termination of Pregnancy services under government regulation. |
| **Govt. Recognised TL Centre** | Tubal Ligation (permanent female sterilisation) under government recognition. |

## 9. What "Everything Under One Roof" Means in Practice

When a patient comes to Vaibhavi Hospital, they have access to:
- OBG consultation and antenatal care
- Emergency obstetric services
- Normal delivery and LSCS
- NICU for the newborn if needed
- ICU if the mother needs intensive care
- All scans and diagnostic tests (same building, Mekhale Diagnostics)
- Post-delivery care and paediatric follow-up
- Vaccination for the newborn

No referral out. No running between labs and clinics.
