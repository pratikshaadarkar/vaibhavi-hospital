# Ideal Patient Profiles — Vaibhavi Hospital

---

> These profiles are built from the discovery call transcript, Dr. Vaibhavi's own words about her patient base, and the geographic/demographic context of Taloja Phase 2, Navi Mumbai. Where assumptions go beyond stated information, they are marked [GAP].

## Profile A — The Pregnant Woman (First-Time or High-Risk)

### Who She Is
- **Age:** 22–35
- **Location:** Taloja Phase 1 & 2, Kalamboli, Kamothe, Kharghar, Pendhar
- **Background:** Middle-class family. Husband likely employed in Navi Mumbai or commutes to Mumbai. May live in a housing society (like Prathmesh Society, the hospital's landmark).
- **Education:** HSC to graduate level. Literate, uses WhatsApp, may search on Google but is not deeply health-literate.
- **First pregnancy or high-risk:** Either a first-time mother navigating unfamiliar territory, or a woman with a previous complication (previous C-section, gestational diabetes, high BP) being transferred from another facility.

### Her Concerns
- "Will my delivery be normal or C-section? How much will each cost?"
- "If something goes wrong with the baby, will we have to be rushed to another hospital?"
- "Is the doctor experienced enough? She looks young."
- "What should I eat? What should I avoid? No one tells me properly."
- "Where do I go for the sonography? Can it be done here only?"

### Decision-Making Process
1. A complication or pregnancy confirmation triggers the need for a gynaecologist
2. She (or her husband) asks neighbours, relatives, or a trusted family member for a recommendation
3. The family scout (often the husband or mother-in-law) visits first to check cost and facilities
4. If cost and first impression are reassuring, the patient is brought in
5. After the first visit with Dr. Vaibhavi, trust is typically established and patients return consistently

### Who Influences Her Choice
- **Primary:** Husband or mother-in-law — they usually make the first enquiry
- **Secondary:** A neighbour or relative who has delivered at a nearby hospital
- **Tertiary:** Google search / hospital board visibility / recommendations from other doctors

### What Trust Looks Like to Her
- Doctor listens to everything without rushing
- Explanation is given clearly — she is told what will happen and why
- She does not have to go anywhere else (everything in one place)
- The facility looks clean, equipped, and credible
- Someone handles her Mediclaim — she doesn't have to figure it out herself

## Profile B — Young Woman with PCOD/PCOS or Menstrual Issues

### Who She Is
- **Age:** 16–30
- **Location:** Taloja, Pendhar, Kalamboli, nearby Navi Mumbai areas
- **Background:** Student or young working woman. May live with parents. Often comes with her mother.
- **Education:** HSC to graduate. Likely found the hospital through Google, Instagram, or word-of-mouth.
- **Situation:** Irregular periods for months or years. May have been told by another doctor she has PCOD but received no follow-up guidance. Possibly stressed, gaining weight, experiencing hair loss or acne — all connected to hormonal imbalance she may not yet understand.

### Her Concerns
- "Is this serious? Will I be able to have children later?"
- "Will I have to take tablets forever?"
- "My periods have been irregular for a year. Is that normal?"
- "I'm embarrassed to talk about this with a male doctor."
- "Can I get treatment here, or will I be referred elsewhere?"

### Decision-Making Process
1. Symptoms become disruptive enough (pain, very irregular cycle, weight gain, anxiety)
2. Mother pushes her to see a doctor, or she searches on Google
3. Preference for a female gynaecologist is strong in this demographic
4. First visit: comes with mother; if Dr. Vaibhavi explains clearly and doesn't dismiss symptoms, patient becomes loyal
5. Treatment adherence depends on ongoing explanation and reassurance

### Who Influences Her Choice
- **Primary:** Her mother — almost always accompanies her on the first visit
- **Secondary:** A friend who has been to this doctor, or a recommendation in a WhatsApp group
- **Tertiary:** Google Maps / nearby location

### What Trust Looks Like to Her
- Female doctor (non-negotiable for many in this demographic)
- Doctor explains the condition in simple language, not just hands over a prescription
- She is not made to feel embarrassed or dismissed
- She understands her treatment plan before leaving the room
- Adolescent counselling is available — she feels this doctor "gets it"

## Profile C — Family Planning / Post-Delivery Care Patient

### Who She Is
- **Age:** 25–40
- **Location:** Taloja and surrounding areas
- **Background:** Married woman, may have 1–2 children. Middle-class. Husband involved in healthcare decisions. May be referred by a relative who delivered at Vaibhavi Hospital, or may have delivered here herself.
- **Situation:** Either looking for a reliable contraceptive option (Copper-T IUCD, family planning counselling), or in post-delivery recovery needing ongoing care for herself and her newborn.

### Her Concerns
- "Which contraceptive is right for me? Will it affect my body?"
- "Will the insertion be painful? Who will do it?"
- "I just delivered — who do I call if something feels wrong at home?"
- "My baby needs vaccinations — can I do everything here itself?"
- "How do I know this is a government-approved place for these procedures?"

### Decision-Making Process
1. Trigger: post-delivery check-up, contraceptive need, or recommendation from a relative
2. Often already a patient of Dr. Vaibhavi from pregnancy — continuity of care is the key factor
3. If new, likely referred by someone who delivered here
4. Govt. recognised MTP & TL status is a reassurance signal — it means the hospital has been vetted

### Who Influences Her Choice
- **Primary:** Her own prior experience with the hospital (if she delivered here)
- **Secondary:** Husband's comfort with the doctor and facility
- **Tertiary:** Word-of-mouth from women in the locality who have used family planning services here

### What Trust Looks Like to Her
- She knows the hospital already — no need to re-establish trust
- Continuity: same doctor, same receptionist, same place
- Govt. recognition reassures her that procedures are safe and regulated
- Mediclaim accepted — less financial anxiety
- Baby vaccination can be done here — one less place to run to
