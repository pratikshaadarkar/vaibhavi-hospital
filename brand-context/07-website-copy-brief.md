## Page 3: Services

### Goal
Make every service feel relevant and approachable — not like a clinical catalogue. Each entry should speak to the patient's concern, not just the procedure name.

### Key Message
*"Whatever you need — we have it here."*

### Copy Direction

Organise services into clear groups. For each group, lead with the patient's concern, then list the services.

**Group 1: Pregnancy Care**
> "From your first scan to your baby's first breath — we are with you."
- ANC Care / Antenatal Care
- High-Risk Pregnancy Management
- Normal Delivery
- Painless Delivery (Epidural)
- Caesarean Section (LSCS)
- Post-Delivery Care

**Group 2: Women's Health**
> "Every stage of a woman's health — treated with expertise and understanding."
- PCOD / PCOS Management
- Menstrual Disorder Treatment
- Colposcopy
- Menopause Management
- Infertility Management
- Adolescent Counselling

**Group 3: Family Planning**
> "Safe, government-recognised family planning services."
- Copper-T / IUCD Insertion
- Family Planning Counselling
- Govt. Recognised MTP Centre
- Govt. Recognised TL Centre

**Group 4: Surgical Services**
- Laparoscopic Surgery
- LSCS / Caesarean Section

**Group 5: Emergency & Critical Care**
> "24/7. Always ready."
- 24/7 Emergency OBG Care
- ICU — Adult Intensive Care
- NICU — Neonatal Intensive Care

**Group 6: Child Health**
- Child Vaccination
- Paediatric Consultations

**Group 7: Diagnostics (Mekhale Diagnostic Center)**
> "All your tests. Same building. No travel."
- Sonography / USG
- Obstetric & Gynaecologic Ultrasound
- Doppler
- X-Ray / Radiology
- Pathology Lab
