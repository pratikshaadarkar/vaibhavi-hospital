## 2. Dr. Vaibhavi Birle — The Person Behind the Hospital

**Qualifications:** MBBS, MS Obstetrics & Gynaecology
**Experience:** 6–7 years post-MS
**Super-specialisation:** None pursued; chose to focus on clinical Obstetrics & Gynaecology
**Family connection to healthcare:** In-laws run Dhanvantari Hospital, Chembur (separate entity, different name)

### Why She Chose Gynaecology
In her own words (from transcript): *"Whenever I studied the subject, I really liked it. It felt very real and practical. It was easy to understand. I also enjoy helping pregnant women, treating them and helping bring newborn babies into the world. This is a very happy and joyful field — most patients are excited about pregnancy and delivery. I like that there is usually a good outcome."*

This is not a doctor who drifted into her specialty. She actively chose Obstetrics because she finds it joyful, human, and outcome-positive.

### Why She Started Her Own Hospital
*"I have wanted this from the beginning. I have always wanted my own hospital, OPD and everything. I have big dreams."*

Founding Vaibhavi Hospital is not a pragmatic pivot — it is the fulfilment of a personal vision she has carried since the start of her career.

### Her Communication Style
Dr. Vaibhavi describes herself as **"not very calm, but very expressive."** She explains things repeatedly until the patient understands. She draws diagrams during consultations. She proactively tells patients what to eat, what to avoid, and what precautions to take — rather than waiting for them to ask.

### A Memorable Story (use for brand storytelling)
During her practice, Dr. Vaibhavi treated a patient for infertility who had been unable to conceive for six months to a year. After treatment, the patient conceived and delivered a baby girl — **and named the child Vaibhavi**, after the doctor. *"That was very emotional for me."*

A second story from her residency: a patient's relative gifted her an entire box of pens after noticing she often lost hers while taking patient vitals through the night. A small gesture — but one that reflects the kind of human connection she builds with patients and their families.

## 3. Hospital Positioning

### Core Positioning Statement
Vaibhavi Hospital is an affordable, full-facility women's and maternity hospital in Taloja Phase 2, where women receive personalised, explained, and compassionate care — without being sent elsewhere for tests, scans, or emergencies.

### Positioning Pillars
1. **Everything Under One Roof** — Patients never need to be transferred. ICU, NICU, diagnostics, pathology, sonography, X-ray, surgery, and OBG are all available in-house or through the co-located Mekhale Diagnostic Center.
2. **Affordable Without Compromise** — OPD fee: ₹400. Sonography: ₹1,100. All major Mediclaim providers accepted. No mention of exact fees on the website — just emphasise affordability.
3. **Doctor Who Explains** — Dr. Vaibhavi's defining trait is that she listens and explains until the patient understands. In a region where patients often feel ignored or rushed, this is a meaningful differentiator.
4. **Government-Recognised Safety** — Govt. recognised MTP & TL centre status signals regulatory compliance and trust.
5. **Friendly & Advanced** — Experienced medical team, modern equipment, warm service.

### Competitive Context
- **Known competitor:** Rana Hospital, Taloja Phase 1 (older, established, patients already know it)
- **Dr. Vaibhavi's honest take:** *"I feel we have more facilities and are more affordable. The only challenge is that people may feel they [Rana] have more experience because they have been around twenty years longer."*
- **Reference inspiration:** Lifeline Hospital, Kamothe (Dr. Vaibhavi's aesthetic benchmark for the website)

## 4. Growth Goals

- **Short-term:** Establish Vaibhavi Hospital as the go-to women's & maternity facility in Taloja Phase 1, Phase 2, Kalamboli, Kamothe, and Kharghar
- **Long-term:** Dr. Vaibhavi's stated personal vision: *"I want to have seven or eight hospitals like this."*
- **Website goal (Dr. Vaibhavi's own words):** *"I want a website that I can show for Mediclaim purposes, and if educated people see it, it should look attractive."*
- **Key metrics to build toward:** Patient volume, Mediclaim panel registrations, Google reviews, referral network

## 5. What the Hospital Should Never Sound Like

- Cold or clinical
- Jargon-heavy
- Corporate / impersonal
- Overly "trusted" — Dr. Vaibhavi noted that "trusted" feels hollow in healthcare: *"Healthcare is not exactly like a service business."*
