About Us

About Vaibhavi Hospital

At Vaibhavi Hospital, we believe every woman deserves care she can understand, in a place she can trust.

Vaibhavi Hospital is Taloja's dedicated women's and maternity hospital, founded by Dr. Vaibhavi Birle with a single conviction: complete women's healthcare should be available close to home, under one roof, led by a female specialist who explains everything. We opened our doors in June 2026 at Ridhisha Tower, Sector 23, Taloja Phase 2.

From routine gynaecological consultations to high-risk pregnancies, ICU and NICU care, laparoscopic surgery, and diagnostics: everything is available inside our building. You arrive with a concern. You leave with clarity and a plan.

As a Government Recognised MTP and TL Centre, we operate with full regulatory compliance and a commitment to safe, ethical care. Our in-house diagnostic partner, Mekhale Diagnostic Centre, ensures your scans and lab reports are ready without a single step outside.

Why Vaibhavi Hospital?

Vaibhavi Hospital was built for the women of Taloja. When Dr. Vaibhavi Birle founded this hospital, she asked one question: what does a woman in Taloja Phase 2 actually need? The answer shaped everything you see here.

A female specialist she can speak to freely. Diagnostics in the same building. An ICU and NICU so her baby stays close. A doctor who explains: draws diagrams, uses plain language, and takes the time you need. That is The Vaibhavi Standard, and it applies to every patient, every time.

Whether you are planning your first pregnancy, managing a PCOD diagnosis, or navigating a high-risk delivery, Vaibhavi Hospital is ready. Walk in, call ahead, or visit us every Thursday for our free community health camp.
