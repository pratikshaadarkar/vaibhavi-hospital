## Section 1 — HERO

### Visual Style & Emotional Palette

**Photography:** ⚠️ REAL PHOTO NEEDED — Dr. Vaibhavi in consultation, looking directly at patient (not camera). Warm natural light. Green scrubs or professional white coat. Clean clinical room with soft warm tones — not sterile fluorescent white. No stock images.

**Color application:**
- Background: Brand Green `#39892F` bleeds into white — not a hard block
- Overlay: Subtle white gradient on right half for text legibility
- Badge accent: Brand Blue `#00338E` for the Govt. Recognised marker

### Silent Authority Signals

- **No CTA button in the hero.** The woman lands. She reads the doctor's name. She sees the credentials. She sees the address — she recognises "behind Pendhar Metro." Trust is planted before she's asked to do anything.
- **Govt. Recognised MTP & TL Centre badge** sits quietly below the credential line. It doesn't shout — it signals compliance and safety to anyone who knows what it means.
- **Address as landmark, not bureaucratic text.** "Behind Pendhar Metro Station" is how local patients navigate — use their language.

## Section 2 — 24/7 TRUST STRIP

*Full-width strip, immediately below hero fold. Calm, not alarming.*

### Visual Style & Emotional Palette

**Background:** Brand Green `#39892F` solid — but muted, not electric. A thin strip, not a billboard.
**Typography:** White, clean sans-serif. Icon + text inline. Low visual noise.
**Emotional palette:** Quiet readiness. "We're here" — not "EMERGENCY AVAILABLE NOW."

### Layout Hierarchy

```

**Copy:** "Open 24/7 for emergencies. OPD morning and evening — walk in or call ahead."

**Design note:** This strip answers the silent question every anxious patient has: *"But what if something happens at night?"* It answers it passively, without dramatising it.

```

**Scroll logic:** The woman lands. She sees the doctor — her face, her name, her credentials. She understands what's here. She sees the doctor's story. She reads what other women said. By the time she reaches the CTA, the decision has already been made. The button just confirms it.
